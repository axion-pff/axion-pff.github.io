MODULE 01
AVAILABLE

Phase-field fracture

The AT2 and PF-CZM models share one element definition. Staggered displacement–damage solves, bound-constrained damage, the spectral tension–compression split, element-by-element Jacobi-preconditioned conjugate gradients, refinement within fixed-size arrays and a multi-GPU cell decomposition — verified on two- and three-dimensional benchmarks and applied to hydraulic fracture in a poroelastic medium, a premolar under contact loading and a concrete gravity dam.

Next steps

Where the framework is going

Directions that follow from the limitations of the present implementation.

DERIVATIVES
NEXT

Reverse-mode derivatives

Forward-mode derivatives are available today. Implicit differentiation of each converged increment would provide reverse-mode derivatives for inverse identification, optimization and uncertainty quantification.

SOLVERS
NEXT

Scalable preconditioning

Linear systems are solved by Jacobi-preconditioned conjugate gradients, whose iteration count grows with mesh refinement. A scalable preconditioner such as multigrid would allow finer meshes and larger device counts.

DAMAGE SOLVER
NEXT

Convergent PF-CZM damage solves

The projected Jacobi iteration for the non-convex PF-CZM damage subproblem can stall during crack propagation. A solver for indefinite, bound-constrained problems, such as a primal–dual active-set method, would make such results quantitative.

ADAPTIVITY
NEXT

Adaptive refinement on several GPUs

The octree refinement addresses box-shaped domains, refines without coarsening and runs on one device. Combining it with the multi-GPU decomposition is the next step.

AI × PHYSICS
NEXT

Training data for AI

High-fidelity solutions at moderate cost make the solver a source of training data for neural operators and other surrogate models.
